What Happens Next

Here's a simple guide to the process from here to your mortgage offer.

1

We'll Call You to Discuss Your Requirements

One of our expat mortgage specialists will call you to have an initial conversation about your situation — where you're based, your income currency, the property you're looking at, and your deposit amount. Can't wait? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll speak to you right away.

2

We Search the Market for Your Best Deal

Using the information you've provided, we compare rates and products across our full panel of lenders who specialise in expat mortgages. We'll present you with the best available options and explain the pros and cons of each.

3

Agreement in Principle

Once you've chosen the right product, we'll secure an Agreement in Principle (AIP) from the lender. This gives you a clear confirmation of what you can borrow and puts you in a strong position when making an offer on a property.

4

Full Mortgage Application

We handle the full mortgage application on your behalf — gathering the required documents, liaising directly with the lender, and managing the process through to your formal mortgage offer.

5

Mortgage Offer & Completion

Once your mortgage offer is received, we'll guide you through to exchange and completion alongside your solicitor — making sure everything goes smoothly right to the end.
