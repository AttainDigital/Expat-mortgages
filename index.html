Why mortgage London?

Mortgage London's expertise is in helping clients who live and work overseas navigate the UK mortgage market.

Our customers like the advice we offer, when they ask:

  • • How will this work when I live and work overseas?

  • • I'm paid in a foreign currency, is that an issue?

  • • I have no UK credit history, is that an issue?

  • • How much can I borrow?

  • • How much deposit will I need?

We provide straight answers to all your questions. There is no obligation and no upfront fee.
