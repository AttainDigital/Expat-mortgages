Why Mortgage London for US Expats?

Mortgage London specialises in helping American citizens living and working abroad secure UK mortgages — including clients with FATCA obligations, USD income, and no UK credit history.

We have the answers when our US clients ask:

  • • Does my US citizenship affect my eligibility for a UK mortgage?

  • • I earn in US dollars — will UK lenders accept that?

  • • I don't have a UK credit history — can I still get approved?

  • • How does FATCA affect my mortgage application?

  • • How much can I borrow and how much deposit will I need?

We provide straight answers to all your questions. There is no obligation and no upfront fee.
